--- Re-shipping the fully completed version of this project ---

This website is a catalogue of all things related to the kpop group Alpha Drive One, including their discography, variety show apperances and TV shows.
Right now I only have the simple framework of it done and a section featuring all of the members! 

I made this to give back to my fandom and have a space on the internet where they can find content that would be extremely hard to find through regular google searches, I also made it so that people who are new to the group can learn more about the members, discography and find content they enjoy :D
Project is mostly just a rando page on the internet so if you click on the vercel link it should work.

Going back to the main page, the main feature of this project is the database which can be accessed by clicking the "allyz" option (thats our fandom name!)

<img width="1509" height="889" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7e7f2784-c70c-4633-a380-971f58b76890" />

You'll see a netflix esque UI with 4 different categories consisting of variety, shows, lives, and more. This is to easily filter and categorize the content :D (By the way variety is categorized by korean variety shows which are similar to game shows). All of the content is filtered by most recent. 

You can scroll by moving left or right with your trackpad! If you don't have a trackpad... that's a feature for later!!

If you're looking for a specific video/show you can use the search bar at the top, searches are also filtered by category too. 

I also wanted to make sure that this project was accessible for people to update since I don't want to be inputting stuff on the daily with an array. This is why I made a contributor function as well! 
Theres a small (+) button at the very bottom right corner of the screen, upon clicking that you will be welcomed with this pop up:

<img width="374" height="469" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5a3166ff-db9a-43eb-97db-000aa96b2a8d" />

The silly pop up will ask you for a few things such as the release date of the content so it can be easily filtered, the title and a short description as well as the category it's in. Also what I decided to do for this database was, because it's a huge hassle to keep downloading photos and uploading them to the database, I actually found a way to have display images by pasting the image UI, since most videos are in landscape format and don't need any serious resizing. After pressing the "add item" button, a little toaster notification will pop up thanking you for contributing to this project. 

The final option on the home page is ald1flix, and it basically just tells the user about who I am and the developer behind the project, also if they want to contribute to the project too (userauth is a beta feature I don't want to do that maybe ill just have a password)
